# VectorByte_projectCali

##NW California Mosquito Dynamics: Drought vs. Normal Years

##A VecDyn-based analysis by Evan Behmer Porter, Hilary McLeland‑Wieser, Molly Radosevich and Mayank Gangwar

###Project Overview

This project analyzes long‑term mosquito population data from Northwest California, sourced from the VecDyn database (222, ), to understand how extreme drought conditions (2015–2017) altered mosquito abundance, seasonality, and community composition compared to climatologically “normal” years.
California’s 2015–2017 drought was one of the most severe on record, and its ecological impacts on mosquito dynamics. 
The data is available from 2013 to 2023, where samples are in individuals per trap per day for Riverside County, California, USA.
By combining multiple datasets from VecDyn repo to form a input dataset, we will utilize ecological time‑series methods, climate covariates, and exploratory modeling. We aims to quantify how drought reshapes mosquito population trajectories and potential vector‑borne disease risk.

###Objectives
1. Compare mosquito abundance across drought vs. non‑drought years- Identify shifts in total abundance, peak timing, and seasonal duration

2. Assess environmental drivers- Link mosquito dynamics to precipitation, temperature, and humidity
- Evaluate lagged climate effects on mosquito emergence
- Explore whether drought alters climate–mosquito relationships

3. Characterize community‑level changes
- Diversity and evenness metrics
- Species turnover between drought and normal periods


4. Build exploratory models
- Time‑series decomposition 

- Drought‑year vs. normal‑year predictive models

###Data Sources

- VecDyn mosquito abundance time series for NW California
- Climate data (ERA5)

### Planned Analyses

Exploratory Data Analysis
- Temporal trends and seasonal curves
- Heatmaps of abundance across years
- Correlation structures between climate and mosquito counts
- Cross‑correlation and lag analysis with climate variables

Visualization Outputs
- Drought vs. normal year abundance curves
